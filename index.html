Group-buying platform for a supermarket chain

Freelance

Customers band together on a product at a supermarket retailer: the more who join, the lower the price per person. If the group reaches its minimum before the deadline, the purchase goes through at the tier price everyone unlocked — if not, nobody is charged.

  • Clean / hexagonal architecture — domain logic has zero framework dependencies, enforced by an ArchUnit build gate
  • Price tiers, join rules and deadline outcomes computed from a time port, never stored or swept by a scheduler
  • Optimistic locking on concurrent joins, stateless JWT auth, server-side filtered/sorted/paged listing
  • WCAG AA accessibility and full test pyramid: unit, property-based (jqwik), architecture, Testcontainers, API, Vitest, Playwright e2e
